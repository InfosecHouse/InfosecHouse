Infosec House is part of the community it serves, and we put support back into it. Partnerships are how we build brand recognition and public visibility while giving back: over the years we've sponsored conferences, backed CTF teams, and run giveaways for the people who make this field what it is.

## What we support

- **Conferences and meetups.** Helping security events happen.
- **CTF teams and competitions.** Backing teams and the events they compete in.
- **Giveaways.** Tools and accounts for the community, like Shodan memberships.
- **Open-source projects.** Tools and resources that fit the Infosec House mission.

## Let's work together

Running a CTF, organizing a conference, or building a project that fits the community? We'd love to hear about it. Tell us what you're working on and how we can help.
