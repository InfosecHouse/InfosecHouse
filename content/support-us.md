## Sponsorship

Reach a focused security audience. Infosec House serves over 100,000 requests a month to more than 7,000 unique visitors, which makes it a good place to put your security tool or business in front of practitioners.

| Tier | What you get |
| :--- | :--- |
| Bronze | A "Sponsor" badge on your tool's listing so visitors know you back the project. |
| Silver | Everything in Bronze, plus a banner at the top of the category page most relevant to you. |
| Gold | Everything in Silver, plus a shout-out to your product across our social media. |
| Diamond | Everything in Gold, plus your logo in the site footer, shown on every page across Infosec House. |

Want the current rate card? Email [sales@infosec.house](mailto:sales@infosec.house). See our [current sponsors](/sponsors/).

### How we handle sponsorships

Sponsored placements, the banners, footer logos, and the "Sponsor" badge on a sponsor's own listing, are always clearly labeled as such. Everything else stays editorial: sponsorship never changes a tool's position in the catalog (listings are sorted alphabetically for everyone), never rewrites a tool's description, and never gets a competitor removed. That independence is the only reason a spot here is worth anything.

Infosec House also gives back: we sponsor conferences, CTF teams, and community giveaways. If you're organizing something that fits, see [Partnership](/partnership/).
